## Design Context

### Users
Chinese-speaking developers, engineers, and technology enthusiasts who want a single place to discover and access curated Chinese-language tech newsletters across domains such as AI, frontend, Go, Python, Rust, cloud-native, and design.

### Brand Personality
Curated, trustworthy, editorial — like a well-typeset printed magazine collection that respects the reader's time and attention.

### Aesthetic Direction
Classic Print style: warm paper tones, refined serif headings paired with clean sans-serif body text, structured grids, generous whitespace, and subtle ornamental details. The interface should feel like a carefully edited periodical rather than a generic dashboard.

### Design Principles
1. **Readability first**: body text no smaller than 14px, line-height 1.6–1.8, Chinese text left-aligned.
2. **Restrained palette**: use beige, light gray, deep blue, ink green, brown, black, and white; accent with warm orange, deep blue, or natural green; never use purple.
3. **Clear typographic hierarchy**: serif for headings, sans-serif for body and UI labels.
4. **Subtle interactions**: hover effects should be gentle — slight shadow or border changes, not animations for their own sake.
5. **Content-forward**: cards and navigation should present newsletter names, descriptions, latest posts, and update times without visual noise.
